📝 Methodological note: 📊 The data present the monthly and annual changes of the Harmonized Index of Consumer Prices (HICP). The historical series for the period January 2016 - April 2026 has been harmonized and recalculated according to the international classification COICOP 2018 and with base year 2025 = 100, in accordance with Eurostat methodological standards. 🔄 The implementation of the new COICOP 2018 classification and base year 2025 = 100 started with the publication for January 2026. 📈 The monthly change represents price movements compared to the previous month, while the annual change represents the change compared to the same month of the previous year. 🏛️ Source: Kosovo Agency of Statistics (KAS).)
